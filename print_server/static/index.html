Upload a photo here to print it on instant film!

Share some fun memories from today or from the past in the guestbook. Just submit your photo below and it will be printed in seconds from the printer near the guestbook.

Photo upload

Tap "Select photo" to open a file selection prompt, chose one image to upload then zoom and pan to crop the final image to the correct size. Tap "Upload" to print the cropped selection of the photo.

Troubleshooting

The printer only holds up to 10 film at a time, if it runs out of film the LEDs will flash yellow.

You'll be able to find more film in a basket next to the printer. There are instructions for loading film on the packets.

You can still upload photos while the printer is out of film or offline, uploaded photos will be kept in a queue until the printer is online again.

If you encounter any problems uploading a photo, find Josh and let them know what's going wrong.
