Upload a photo here to print it on instant film!

Share some fun memories from today or from the past in the guestbook. Submit your photo below and it will be printed from the printer near the guestbook!

Photo upload

Tap "Select photo" to open a file selection prompt, chose one photo to upload then zoom and pan to crop the final image to the correct size. Tap "Upload" to send the cropped selection of the photo to the server, then wait a few moments for your photo to be printed.

Troubleshooting

The printer can hold up to 10 film at a time, if it runs out the icon at the top of the page will be red and read will "Printer is out of film!".

You'll find more film in a basket next to the printer. If the printer is empty, feel free to load it, or come find me (Josh) to help.

You can still upload photos while the printer is out of film or offline, uploaded photos will be kept in a queue until the printer is online again.

If you encounter any problems uploading a photo, find me (Josh) and I will help :).
